Baytown straddles a county line, and so do its roofing rules

Baytown sits in two counties. The older, western part of the city is Harris County. East of Cedar Bayou, the newer neighborhoods toward Interstate 10 and Mont Belvieu are in Chambers County, and Chambers County is one of the Texas Department of Insurance's designated coastal counties. That line decides whether your re-roof needs a state windstorm inspection.

In the Chambers County part of Baytown, the state windstorm pool, the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association, will only cover a re-roofed house if the work was built to the coastal wind code and checked during installation by a TDI inspector or an appointed engineer. At the end you should receive a WPI-8 certificate of compliance. In the Harris County part of the city, the windstorm program does not apply.

Check your county first. Your appraisal record shows whether you are in Harris or Chambers County. If it is Chambers, make the WPI-8 a written deliverable in your roofing contract.

City permits, even after a storm

Inside city limits, Baytown permits residential roofing work. After Hurricane Beryl in July 2024, the city waived permit fees for storm repairs, but it made a point of saying the permits themselves were still required. That tells you how seriously the city takes them. Your roofing company should pull the permit in its own name. One that suggests skipping it is one to skip.

What Baytown roofs face

Baytown sits where the San Jacinto River meets Galveston Bay, with Trinity Bay not far east. It takes wind from every tropical system that comes up the coast. Hurricane Ike in 2008 damaged roofs across the city, and Beryl lifted shingles and dropped limbs from Lakewood to Chaparral Village. Much of west Baytown was built from the 1940s through the 1970s, so older roofs there often have several layers, board decking and worn flashing. East of Cedar Bayou the houses are newer, many on their first or second roof.

Choosing a Baytown roofing company

  • They ask which county you are in and handle the windstorm inspection when you are in Chambers.
  • They pull the city permit. Even when fees are waived.
  • Proof they are covered. Ask for their liability and workers' compensation certificate. With no state license for roofers, that paper matters.
  • Everything on paper. Old layers off to the deck, the underlayment brand, replacement flashing, what each bad sheet of decking will cost, and how long they stand behind the labor.
  • Your deductible is yours. Coastal deductibles can be large, but Texas law requires you to pay it, and a roofer offering to waive it is breaking the law.

Common questions

Which Baytown homes need a windstorm certificate for a new roof?

Homes on the Chambers County side, east of Cedar Bayou. Chambers is one of the designated coastal counties, so TWIA expects a WPI-8 after a re-roof there. Houses on the Harris County side are outside the program.

Do I need a city permit to re-roof in Baytown?

Yes, inside city limits. After Hurricane Beryl the city waived storm-repair permit fees but said the permits were still required.

How do I know which county my Baytown house is in?

Look up your property on the Harris Central Appraisal District or Chambers County Appraisal District site. Only one of them will have your address.

Are roofers licensed in Texas?

No. Texas does not license roofers at all. Lean on their insurance certificate, the city permit, the WPI-8 where it applies, and roofs they have done nearby.

What parts of Baytown can call this line?

Every part of Baytown, from Lakewood, Chaparral Village and the neighborhoods along Garth Road and Baker Road to the east side toward Interstate 10, plus Mont Belvieu, Highlands and Crosby.
