Humble the city, and Humble the address

The City of Humble is a small place, about seventeen thousand people around Main Street and the old oil field. The Humble mailing address covers far more: Atascocita, much of the Lake Houston shoreline, Eagle Springs, Walden on Lake Houston, Fall Creek and the neighborhoods along FM 1960 and the Beltway. Most of those are unincorporated Harris County, and some are inside the City of Houston's limits.

The rules follow the line. Inside the City of Humble, a roof replacement goes through the city's Building and Inspection Department, which uses a permit fee of fifty dollars plus a small charge per square foot. In unincorporated Harris County there is no building permit for an ordinary re-roof, so no inspector comes out. Ask a Humble roofer which side of the line you are on before you sign; a company that works here will know.

Roofs near Lake Houston and the San Jacinto

Much of the Humble area sits along Lake Houston and the forks of the San Jacinto River, under tall pines and hardwoods. That brings the wear you see on so many roofs here:

  • Shade and damp. Shaded slopes near the water stay wet, grow algae and let decking soften under shingles that still look fine.
  • Needles and leaves. Pine straw packs into valleys and gutters and holds water against the flashing.
  • Limbs. Hurricane Beryl in July 2024 brought trees down across northeast Harris County, and a large limb can go straight through decking.

A good roofer looks up at the trees before quoting and tells you which limbs over the roof should come down first.

What separates Humble roofing companies

  • They pull the permit when you are inside the city, in the company's name, and they know when you are not.
  • A certificate of insurance, sent to you. Texas has no state roofing license, so liability and workers' compensation coverage is your main protection.
  • Addresses nearby. Roofs they did in Atascocita, Kingwood or Humble last year that you can drive past.
  • A scope in writing. Tear-off to the deck, the underlayment by name, new drip edge, flashing and pipe boots, a per-sheet price for decking, and a workmanship warranty.
  • No deductible games. Texas law requires you to pay your deductible. A roofer who offers to waive or absorb it is breaking the law.

Repair, replace, or claim

One leaking pipe boot or a handful of missing shingles is a repair at any age. A roof past fifteen years with granules in the gutters and leaks in more than one room usually needs replacing. Common questions

Do I need a roofing permit in Humble, TX?

Inside the City of Humble, yes: re-roofs go through the city's Building and Inspection Department. Most homes with a Humble address, including Atascocita, are in unincorporated Harris County, where an ordinary re-roof needs no building permit.

Is Atascocita part of Humble?

No. Atascocita has a Humble mailing address but is unincorporated Harris County, so City of Humble permit rules do not apply there.

Why do Humble-area roofs grow black streaks?

That is algae, which grows on shaded, damp roof slopes under trees and near Lake Houston. Algae-resistant shingles slow it down on a replacement.

Are roofers licensed in Texas?

No. There is no state roofing license, so insurance, local references and a clear written contract are what protect you.
