## Search evidence (E1 to E24)
- E1 galvestontx.gov/162: "WPI-8 from TDI is required upon completion of all new construction, solar panels, siding and roofing replacement."
- E2 TDI inspection process / tips pages: notify TDI before work (WPI-1); WPI-8 for ongoing construction by TDI inspector or appointed engineer; WPI-8-E for completed work by a Texas licensed PE; roof repairs under 100 sq ft not involving decking exempt; windstorm index has the certificate lookup.
- E3 Galveston Landmark Commission staff reports: metal allowed when detailed compatibly; replacement similar in size, shape, texture, color where documented; retain/repair original roofing; denials occur.
- E4 FEMA TB 8 and P-2181 FS 3.3.1: salt spray greatest near breaking waves, declines rapidly in first 300 to 3,000 ft; hot-dip galvanized or stainless within 3,000 ft; mechanically galvanized fasteners discouraged for exterior use (no "more brittle" language found).
- E5 Ike: NHC TCR: upper end of Category 2 at landfall, Bolivar/Chambers surge 15 to 20 ft by ground teams (gauges failed), highest HWM 17.5 ft about 10 n mi inland in Chambers County. NWS FWD page alone gives 10.8 ft / 109 mph at Galveston (conflicts with NHC 10 to 15 ft estimate).
- E6 Fox Weather: Galveston top gust 86 mph and FOUR straight hours of 70 mph gusts (page said three).
- E7 Galveston electric: CenterPoint is the island's utility (Galveston Daily News, Houston Public Media coverage of outages 2024 to 2026); TNMP serves the mainland county; Entergy serves Bolivar Peninsula.
- E8 Galveston local historic districts pamphlet and district pages: East End 1971 ~66 blocks; Silk Stocking 1975 (19 blocks; 25th Street/Rosenberg the core); Lost Bayou 1994, ~23 blocks, Hitchcock's Bayou filled in 1880s.
- E9 La Porte FAQ QID=182 and TID=32: permit needed even to reroof your own home; correct shingle type critical; CSS portal; Inspections 281-470-5073, 604 W Fairmont Pkwy.
- E10 La Porte: Lomax consolidated by election April 5 1980; district map "Lomax area (everything west of SH 146 and north of Spencer Highway)".
- E11 TWIA Instructions and Guidelines Manual (2022): La Porte 1-1-1988 to 3-1-1996 in the building code / building official statement table (Seabrook same; Pasadena and Shoreacres to 3-1-97).
- E12 Harris County Historical Commission Sylvan Beach Pavilion marker: 1930s pavilion destroyed by tornado spawned by 1943 hurricane; county bought park 1954; hurricane-resistant pavilion opened May 1956.
- E13 La Porte CivicAlerts AID=325: 97,000 cubic yards of debris, largest debris incident since Ike; working with CenterPoint on reliability.
- E14 Click2Houston Nov 1 2015: tornado damaged at least 70 La Porte homes.
- E15 La Porte /1370: no official contractor registration process, form or fee; COI naming City of La Porte as certificate holder; permits@laportetx.gov.
- E16 Pasadena Fee Schedule: roof replacement and/or repairs over 25 percent of a roof require permit; flat residential fee per structure for overlay/like materials with minor decking; commercial engineered drawings when structurally altered; wood shingles/shakes/combustible roofing prohibited except minor repairs to existing single-family homes.
- E17 TDI B-0024-97 and Order 96-1468 (Dec 18 1996): parts of Pasadena and Shoreacres east of SH 146 added effective March 1 1997.
- E18 Pasadena Permits page and directory: 8 to 5 at City Hall, no permits after 4:30 p.m.; Permit Department 713-475-5575, 1149 Ellsworth Dr.
- E19 Baytown "When is a Residential Permit Required" and Building Services list: shingle replacement only, no permit; roof deck installation/replacement/repair requires one; "NOT a complete list"; Fire Marshal inspects tar pot roofing operations.
- E20 Baytown blog IID=165 / CivicAlerts 685: Beryl storm repair permit fees waived, permits still required; new ordinance waives fees automatically for 30 days after the mayor signs a local disaster declaration; council may extend.
- E21 Brownwood: Alicia destroyed it; home count conflicts (360+ vs ~500 in different sources).
- E22 HGSD: created by Legislature 1975; Brownwood abandoned in 1980s after more than nine feet of subsidence; USGS: Baytown-Pasadena up to 12 to 13 ft historically, largely abated.
- E23 Handbook of Texas (Pelly) / Texas Almanac: Goose Creek and Pelly (with old Baytown) consolidated 1947, named Baytown Jan 1948. Baytown mostly Harris County, Chambers part east of Cedar Bayou (THC atlas, HCFCD Cedar Bayou).
- E24 Baytown EDF: CenterPoint Energy serves the City of Baytown and Chambers County; CenterPoint service-center list.
- E25 HCFCD: Vince Bayou watershed (Vince and Little Vince) mostly in Pasadena and South Houston; Armand Bayou includes parts of Pasadena.
