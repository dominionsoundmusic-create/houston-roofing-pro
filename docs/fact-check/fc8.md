## Search evidence (E1 to E21)
- E1 City of Humble history page (cityofhumbletx.gov/humble-history/): Pleasant Humble ran a ferry near today's US 59 crossing; post office in his home 1886, Humble name adopted; incorporated 1933. Wikipedia: Humble field largest producing field in Texas by 1905; Humble Oil founded 1911.
- E2, E4 West Fork gauge: NO source found for 69.18 ft (Aug 29 2017) or 66.39 ft (Oct 1994). Community Impact quotes a Humble official calling the 1994 record 67.3 ft at the US 59 gauge (conflicts), and during Harvey reported the US 59 gauge at 62 ft and forecast to keep rising.
- E3 "16,000 homes and 3,300 businesses": Lake Houston area chamber estimate, reported in 2018 dredging coverage; the City dredging update PDF (sjr-dredging-update-20180724.pdf) appears in results for the exact figures.
- E5 Kingwood annexation: Houston annexation history (1995 and 1996); TIRZ 10 page: area annexed Dec 11 1996, zone set up to replace MUDs absorbed in that annexation. Forest Cove: NOT found.
- E6 City planning profile (Kingwood_Area_Final.pdf): commercial entrance annexed 1995, residential 1996; heavily wooded; wide range of home types; most residents in Humble ISD; small part in Montgomery County (New Caney ISD).
- E7 HCFCD news release: Kingwood area flooded May 2019 and again September 2019.
- E8 Electric utility: aggregator pages (electricrates.org, ComparePower) list Kingwood, Humble and Atascocita in CenterPoint territory; no Entergy mention. Deer Park and Channelview are inside CenterPoint's Houston core (also sister fc7 for Channelview).
- E9 Atascocita: Wikipedia: name from Atascocito outpost/road (Spanish, 1750s), construction began 1970s, 2020 census 88,174.
- E10 Eagle Springs (Community Impact, Lake Houston area, 77346) and Walden on Lake Houston (western shore of Lake Houston, Atascocita corridor; real estate guides): both confirmed as Atascocita-area subdivisions.
- E11 tshaonline.org: entries exist for Atascocita (northeastern Harris County, rapid 1990s growth), Humble (incorporated 1933), Channelview (named for its location on the northeastern curve of the Ship Channel; refinery workers after 1916 oil discoveries), Atascosito Road. No Deer Park entry.
- E12 City of Humble building inspection page: city has a permit portal, "not all permits are available for online submission". Official domain is cityofhumbletx.gov (page cited cityofhumble.net).
- E13 HCFCD F-15 Atascocita Area Drainage Analysis: planning study of riverine flood risk around Atascocita; feasibility contract Feb 2022.
- E14 HCFCD Carpenters Bayou: about 25 sq mi, parallels East Sam Houston Tollway, passes through Channelview to the Ship Channel; lower end tidally influenced, vulnerable to surge. URL is /Activity/Projects/Carpenters-Bayou.
- E15 City of Deer Park 2019 Roof Permit sheet: residential roof permit via Planning and Development Services, 710 E San Augustine, 281-478-7270; 30 lb felt, Class F shingles, six nails per shingle; $60 fee and $45 reinspection (2019, not used).
- E16 City of Deer Park history page: Simeon H. West settled 1892; Shell arrived 1928; incorporated Dec 10 1948 (Wikipedia gives the Dec 12 vote).
- E17 Channelview neighborhoods (Redfin): Old River Terrace, Cedar Bluff, Sterling Green, Old River Acres, Sonoma Ranch.
- E18 Deer Park neighborhoods (HAR.com): Deer Park Gardens median year built 1971; Deer Meadows 1975.
- E19 Houston Public Works May 23 2024 release and HPC fast-track page: re-roof and overlay permits expedited after the derecho; confirm deed restriction compliance.
