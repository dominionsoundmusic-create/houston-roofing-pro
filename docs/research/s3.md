# Research notes: writer s3 (commercial roofing, flat roof repair, metal roofing), Oct 12 to 13 2026

Method: WebSearch only (WebFetch/curl to tdi.texas.gov and wbdg.org failed with DNS errors). Facts below were
read in search-result excerpts from the named URL. The shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through, so
anything I could not confirm was left off the pages (listed at the end).

## /commercial-roofing/
- City of Houston commercial roofing permit, HPWCODE1157: covers a new roof or overlay on a commercial structure;
  plan review and inspection required; 6 month validity; processing 10 business days; admin fee $33.56 (fee not
  used on page); documents: building permit application plus Commercial Re-Roof Only Worksheet CE-1076 or
  Roofing Overlay worksheet CE-1104, plans if applicable; approvals may include Structural, Planning, Fire
  Marshal, Flood Plain; permit, worksheet and plans kept on site. https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/hpwcode1157
- Houston re-roofing guidance: an Energy Code Software Report with completed COMcheck inspection checklist is
  required for changes to deck insulation during re-roofing. https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/media/10371/download
  (page says "energy code compliance report with a completed inspection checklist"; fact-checker should
  confirm this PDF is the document carrying that line).
- Low-slope = slope 3:12 or less; system families BUR, mod bit, single-ply (TPO, PVC, EPDM), fluid-applied.
  https://wbdg.org/node/399
- Single-ply EPDM, PVC, TPO can be fully adhered, mechanically fastened or ballasted. State of Connecticut DAS
  handout https://portal.ct.gov/-/media/DAS/OEDM/2018-CD-HO/FA18--Roofing-Flashing-and-Related-Materials-3-Slide-Handouts.pdf
- TPO and PVC heat-welded seams; EPDM adhesive or tape seams; mod bit is polymer-modified asphalt sheets; BUR
  multiple plies. https://roofingcontractor.com/articles/98835-choosing-the-best-low-slope-roofing-system-for-your-building
- NRCA: maintenance inspections at least biannually, preferably spring and fall, plus after severe weather
  (hurricane, tornado, hailstorm). https://professionalroofing.net/articles/article/401
- NRCA: positive drainage / proper slope = no ponding 48 hours after rain in drying conditions.
  https://www.nrca.net/RoofingGuidelines/Glossary/detail/521 ; https://professionalroofing.net/articles/article/2109
- ENERGY STAR citing LBNL: clean white roof reflecting 80% stays about 50 F cooler than gray roof reflecting 20%
  on a typical summer afternoon. https://www.energystar.gov/products/building_products/roof_products/cool_roofs_emissivity
- TDI FAQ: commercial structure insured for wind/hail through TWIA must at minimum be built to TDI's adopted
  building standards. https://tdi.texas.gov/WIND/generalquestio.html
- TWIA: improvements needing certification include repairs (including reroofing). https://www.twia.org/windstorm-certification
- TDI product evaluation requirements: formed metal panels tested to UL 1897, UL 580, ASTM E 1592 or FM 4474.
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/WIND/documents/prodevalreqRC.pdf
- Derecho May 16 2024 (NWS HGX May 2024 summary; NOAA NESDIS) and Beryl July 8 2024 (Community Impact): same
  sources as /roof-repair/.

## /flat-roof-repair/
- NRCA 48-hour ponding criterion (above). The IRC R202 "positive roof drainage" wording (drain within 48 hours of
  precipitation) could not be read in the ICC text itself, so the page attributes the 48 hours to NRCA only.
- IRC R905: modified bitumen and single-ply need minimum design slope 1/4 in 12 (2%); reroofing exception for
  roofs that provide positive drainage (quoted in IIBEC Technical Advisory 015).
  https://iibec.org/publication-post/iibec-technical-advisory-no-015-2017-secondary-drainage-provisions/
- City of Houston residential: roof covering up to 100 sq ft aggregate exempt; re-roof/overlay needs residential
  roofing permit. https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/hpwcode1140 (verified by coordinator in WRITING-GUIDE).
- Seam methods per material and ENERGY STAR/LBNL figure: sources above.
- Repair steps, blisters, edges, drains: described as general roofing practice and questions to ask, without
  statistics.

## /metal-roofing/
- TDI blog, 4 key facts: discount not offered by every insurer and varies; metal can be dented by hail, debris,
  branches; discount may come with conditions such as a cosmetic damage exclusion; terms can change at renewal.
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/blog/metal-roofs-and-home-insurance-4-key-facts-for-homeowners.html
- TDI Bulletin B-0030-98: optional endorsement HO-145 (and TDP-022) excluding cosmetic hail damage to roof
  coverings on risks receiving a premium credit for UL 2218 impact-resistant roofing; cosmetic = appearance
  change not allowing water penetration or failure to perform; void unless signed by insured.
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/bulletins/1998/b-0030-8.html
- TWIA manual (2023): Cosmetic Exclusion endorsement (TWIA-420) attached to residential policies.
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/rules/2023/documents/twiainstruc0422.pdf
- Texas Property Code 202.011 (HB 362, 2011): POA may not prohibit owner otherwise allowed shingles from
  installing shingles designed to be wind/hail resistant, more energy efficient, or solar generating, if they
  resemble allowed shingles, are equal or more durable, and match surrounding aesthetics. Text is about
  shingles; page says metal panels are "not clearly covered". https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_202.011
- LBNL 2004 cool colors: cool brown metal about 27% reflectance vs about 8% conventional brown.
  https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2004/08/27/cool-colors-cool-roofs/
- TDI RC-678 shows uplift design pressure varying with clip spacing (-52.5 psf at 18 in to -86.0 psf at 6 in).
  https://tdi.texas.gov/Wind/prod/rc/rc678.pdf (numbers not used on page; only the principle).
- Gauges: lower number thicker; 22/24/26/29 common; 24 for heavy hail areas, 26 moderate, 29 sheds/outbuildings.
  https://www.angi.com/articles/metal-roofing-gauges.htm (fact-checker: confirm the 24/26/29 guidance is Angi's
  and not the other dealer page in the same search).
- Exposed-fastener panels lower cost, fasteners need checking/tightening; standing seam uses concealed clips,
  harder to repair. https://blog.mcelroymetal.com/metal-roofing-and-siding/through-fastened-vs.-standing-seam-metal-roofing-a-comparison
- Noise: solid deck + underlayment + insulated attic about as quiet as shingles (manufacturer claim, framed as
  such). https://blog.mcelroymetal.com/metal-roofing-and-siding/are-metal-roofs-noisy-when-it-rains
- Galvalume = aluminum-zinc alloy coating, galvanized = zinc; differ in salt exposure. https://www.fixr.com/articles/galvalume-vs-galvanized-roofs
