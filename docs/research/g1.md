## Roof insurance claims in Texas

| Fact used on the page | Source |
|---|---|
| Replacement cost coverage pays up to the full cost at current prices; actual cash value subtracts depreciation for age and wear. As roofs age some companies switch to ACV at renewal; a roof in poor condition may not be covered at all. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/replacing-your-roof.html |
| Replacement cost claims are usually paid in two checks: first = estimated repair cost minus depreciation and deductible; second = the withheld depreciation after the insurer gets the bill for the finished job; repairs usually must be completed within a set period. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/home-insurance-policies-replacement-cost-or-actual-cash-value.html ; https://www.tdi.texas.gov/pubs/consumer/cb025.html |
| If you have a mortgage, the insurer usually includes the mortgage company on the payment. The insurer might deny a claim if permanent repairs are made before it sees the damage. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/getting-your-insurance-claim-paid.html |
| Percentage deductibles: convert to dollars; TDI example $150,000 home, 5% deductible = $7,500, so a $6,500 repair is entirely under the deductible. Deductible applies to each claim. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/deductibles.html |
| Most companies have deadlines to file a claim; some policies have a one-year deadline unless good cause; TWIA wind and hail: one year from the date of damage. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/replacing-your-roof.html |
| After the insurer receives what it needs, it has 15 business days to decide whether to pay. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/replacing-your-roof.html (search snippet); Insurance Code ch. 542 |
| TWIA: ACV roof endorsement TWIA-400 not available for policies effective on or after Jan 1, 2020; replaced by TWIA-804 (replacement cost on the dwelling, actual cash value on the roof covering). | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/rules/2023/documents/twiainstruc0422.pdf |
| TDI-prescribed optional cosmetic hail damage exclusion endorsement, attachable to policies receiving an impact-resistant roof credit (1998 bulletin; used only to say such exclusions exist). | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/bulletins/1998/b-0030-8.html |
| Impact-resistant discounts set company by company. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/company/roofing-discounts.html |
| Illegal for a contractor to offer to waive, rebate or absorb a deductible (Business and Commerce Code 27.02); contracts of $1,000 or more involving an insurance settlement must include notice the policyholder must pay the deductible; penalties up to a $2,000 fine and up to six months in jail per TDI. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/storms/roofing-and-insurance-know-the-law.html ; https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/can-a-contractor-waive-my-deductible.html |
| Insurance Code ch. 707: policyholder must pay the deductible; insurer may ask for proof (canceled check, money order receipt, credit card statement, copy of a payment plan with the contractor). | same TDI pages |
| Insurance Code 4102.163: a contractor may not act as a public adjuster or advertise to adjust claims on a property where it provides or may provide contracting services; contractors may still give estimates and discuss them and other technical information with the insurer or its adjuster. Public adjusters are licensed by TDI. | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/storms/roofing-and-insurance-know-the-law.html |
| Report deductible offers to the Texas Attorney General Consumer Protection Hotline 800-621-0508. | TDI know-the-law page |
| TDI consumer help line 800-252-3439. | Verified by the coordinator (WRITING-GUIDE.md) |
| TDI contractor scam warning signs page exists (used only as a further-reading source). | https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/storms/know-signs-of-contractor-scam.html |
