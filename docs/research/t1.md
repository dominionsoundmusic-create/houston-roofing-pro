## Katy (/roofing-companies-katy-tx/)
Verified (from search results on the official or news domain):
- City of Katy Permits and Building Services: "permits are required for all new construction, remodeling
  or roofing"; city adopted the 2021 International Building Codes and 2023 NEC.
  https://www.cityofkaty.com/government/city-departments/permits-building
- City of Katy contractor registration: fee $200, valid 12 months from payment; roofing contractor is a
  category on the form; general liability certificate of at least $300,000 naming the City of Katy as
  certificate holder; pre-construction meeting may be required for contractors new to the city.
  https://www.cityofkaty.com/government/city-departments/building-permits/contractor-registration
  https://www.cityofkaty.com/home/showpublisheddocument/9537/638803913107870000
- Permits Office: 901 Avenue C, Katy, TX 77493; 281-391-4830 (same sources).
- April 10, 2024: NWS confirmed brief EF-1 tornado, max winds about 90 mph (NWS HGX MIC Jeffry Evans);
  auto service shop lobby collapsed on S. Mason Road, neighboring sports bar windows blown out; in the
  neighborhood winds est. 60 to 70 mph; CenterPoint about 145,000 customers out at peak (regional).
  https://coveringkaty.com/news/katy/national-weather-service-confirms-tornado-struck-katy/
  https://abc13.com/amp/post/houston-storm-damage-katy-businesses-destroyed-by-high-winds-firestone-collapse-old-mason-road-bourbon-street-sports-bar/14641402/
  https://communityimpact.com/katy-north/weather/early-april-10-tornado-cause-damages-to-katy-businesses/
  (Businesses deliberately not named on the page. 145,000 figure not used.)
- April 5, 2023 hail: quarter to egg size near Franz Rd and N Westgreen Blvd; ping pong size near Falcon
  Landing Blvd (Fort Bend). May 9, 2019: 2.5 inch hail report about 5 miles from Katy.
  https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/katy_texas/all , https://www.stormersite.com/showreport/441087
  (StormerSite compiles NWS HGX reports; a fact-checker should confirm against NOAA Storm Events.)
- Cinco Ranch: land bought Feb 1984 by Cinco Ranch Venture for a 5,000 acre master-planned development;
  Katy area grew into Fort Bend County in the 1990s led by Cinco Ranch; CDP since 2000 census, 16,899 in 2020.
  https://crhs.katyisd.org/our-campus/about-our-namesake , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinco_Ranch,_Texas
- Derecho, Beryl, TDI deductible law, Property Code 202.011, HB 3344: guide-verified facts, same sources
  as the model page.
