## Verified this session (search results)
- TDI Commissioner's Order 96-1468 (stamped Dec 18 1996) designated parts of Pasadena and Shoreacres east
  of SH 146 as catastrophe areas; TDI Bulletin B-0024-97 says effective March 1 1997 (manual amended by
  Order 97-0225 effective April 1 1997). https://tdi.texas.gov/Bulletins/1997/b-0024-7.html ;
  https://tdi.texas.gov/orders/documents/961468cat.pdf
- TDI Harris County windstorm page lists La Porte, Morgan's Point, Pasadena, Seabrook, Shoreacres; Deer
  Park and Channelview not in the excerpt or the guide's list. TDI says maps are general information,
  verify addresses; Windstorm Inspections 1-800-248-6032 option 4. https://tdi.texas.gov/wind/maps/harris.html
- City of Pasadena fee schedule: roof replacement and/or repairs over 25 percent of each roof require
  permits; residential roofing permit $40 per structure incl. application (overlay or like materials, minor
  decking); commercial $50 plan review, engineered drawings when altered, permit fee no less than $250;
  wood shingle/shake/combustible roofing prohibited except minor repairs to existing single-family homes.
  Permit Department at City Hall 1149 Ellsworth, 8 to 5, no permits after 4:30 p.m., 713-475-5575.
  https://www.pasadenatx.gov/2002/Fee-Schedule ; https://www.pasadenatx.gov/399/Permits-Licenses
  (Page says "confirm current fee with the city"; the $40 figure was left OFF the page because the
  schedule's date is unclear.)
- City of Baytown: roof shingle replacement alone listed as NOT requiring a permit; roof deck installation,
  replacement or repair requires one; list "is NOT a complete list"; tar pot roofing goes through the Fire
  Marshal; Citizen Self Service portal; Building Services 281-420-6537.
  https://baytown.org/970/When-is-a-Residential-Permit-Required ;
  https://www.baytown.org/DocumentCenter/View/5358/What-Requires-a-Permit-v11022 ;
  https://www.baytown.org/653/Apply-for-a-Permit ; https://baytown.org/507/Building-Permits
- Baytown July 17 2024 post: permit fees waived for storm damage repairs after Beryl, permits still
  required; new ordinance waives fees automatically for 30 days after the mayor signs a local disaster
  declaration, council may extend. https://www.baytown.org/Blog.aspx?IID=165
- Baytown fee page lists "Roof Replacements $128" but the page is years old and conflicts with the
  shingles-only rule: LEFT OUT.

## From the sister site's confirmed fact-check (fc7.md), primary sources cited on the page
- Baytown in Harris and Chambers counties; Goose Creek, Pelly, Baytown merged late 1940s (TSHA);
  Brownwood ~500 homes devastated by Alicia Aug 1983 (USGS); abandoned in 1980s after >9 ft subsidence,
  now Baytown Nature Center (HGSD); HGSD created 1975.
- Channelview unincorporated (Harris County OCE permits for development: buildings, fill, grading,
  paving); MUDs; Carpenters Bayou ~25 sq mi, beside East Sam Houston Tollway, through Channelview to the
  Ship Channel, lower end tidal and surge-prone (HCFCD); NWS: major river flooding in Harvey including the
  San Jacinto; CenterPoint territory.
- Pasadena drains to Vince, Little Vince and Armand bayous (HCFCD watersheds).
- TDI: homeowners policies do not cover floods; FEMA: separate flood policy, generally 30-day wait.
