## Galveston (/roofing-galveston-tx/)

### City permits and registration
- City of Galveston Building Division page: "WPI-8 from Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) is required
  upon completion of all new construction, solar panels, siding and roofing replacement." The division
  works under the 2021 International codes with amendments.
  https://www.galvestontx.gov/162/Building-Division
- Development Services: building permit applications, contractor registrations and inspection requests
  are submitted online through the ePortal. https://galvestontx.gov/159/Development-Services
- Applications, Fees and FAQs: the property owner is responsible for obtaining required permits;
  construction without a permit can bring double or triple permit fees and possible Municipal Court action;
  Building Division (409) 797-3620. https://www.galvestontx.gov/647/Applications-Fees-and-FAQs
- Contractors and Engineers page lists contractor registration (a $200 fee described as for general
  contractors only, $25 annual). Fee left OFF the page because it is unclear whether it applies to
  roofers. https://www.galvestontx.gov/220/Contractors-Engineers

### Historic districts (city sources)
- Four local historic districts: East End (1971, about 66 blocks), Silk Stocking (1975, 25th Street /
  Rosenberg as the core), Lost Bayou (1994, about 23 blocks, named for Hitchcock's Bayou, filled in the
  1880s), Strand/Mechanic (the only mostly non-residential district).
  https://www.galvestontx.gov/DocumentCenter/Home/View/338 ; https://www.galvestontx.gov/634/Lost-Bayou ;
  https://www.galvestontx.gov/648/Silk-Stocking
- Review is based on the city's Design Standards for Historic Properties, written from the Secretary of
  the Interior's Standards. https://www.galvestontx.gov/1019/Current-Prospective-Historic-Property-Ow
- Landmark Commission staff reports: roof replacements in local districts come to the commission as
  Certificate of Appropriateness requests; keep and repair original roofing that can be repaired; use a
  historically appropriate material similar in size, shape, texture and color to documented original;
  metal allowed when detailed compatibly; composition shingle suggested as an option; rolled roofing only
  on flat roofs; appeal within 10 days. https://galvestontx.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/12413?fileID=30325 ;
  https://www.galvestontx.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/14724?fileID=33581
- Left out: exact current text of the Design Standards (staff reports date 2016 to 2025; page tells
  readers to confirm with the city). Claim from a contractor site about "Galveston Historical Foundation
  review adding 2 to 4 weeks": not official, left out.

### TDI and TWIA
- TDI "What you need to know about windstorm inspections": most roof replacements need an inspection and
  certificate of compliance; small repairs under 100 square feet that do not involve decking are exempt;
  inspection during construction by a TDI inspector or a TDI-appointed licensed engineer; after the fact a
  Texas licensed professional engineer inspects and TDI issues a WPI-8-E; notify TDI before work (form
  WPI-1). https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/need-windstorm-inspection.html
- TDI windstorm index (WPI-8 lookup). https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/index.html
- TWIA: replacing a roof is on the list of improvements needing certification; WPI-8 required for TWIA
  eligibility. https://www.twia.org/windstorm-certification/
- TWIA: do not make permanent repairs such as a roof replacement until the adjuster has inspected.
  https://twia.org/claimscenter/repairs
- Shared guide (verified by coordinator): TWIA claims within one year; deductible law; TDI help line.

### Storms
- Hurricane Ike made landfall at Galveston early on Sept 13, 2008 as a Category 2 hurricane.
  https://weather.gov/shv/event_2008-09-13_Hurricane_Ike
- NWS page on Ike: Galveston saw about a 10.8 foot surge and 109 mph winds; Bolivar Peninsula's surge
  estimated over 17 feet. https://weather.gov/fwd/ikenorthtexas2008 (label kept free of other metros)
- Hurricane Beryl (July 8, 2024): Galveston top gust 86 mph and three straight hours of 70 mph gusts
  (Fox Weather). https://foxweather.com/weather-news/beryl-landfall-texas-hurricane
- NWS Houston/Galveston Beryl summary: numerous trees and power lines down; highest gust 97 mph near the
  Brazos River in Brazoria County. https://www.weather.gov/media/hgx/TropicalEventSummary/PSHHGX_2024AL02_Beryl_Summary.pdf
- Left out: any Galveston roof-damage count for Beryl (none found; AP reported no immediate widespread
  structural damage). The 1900 storm and grade raising: could not verify within the search budget, left out.

### Salt air
- FEMA Technical Bulletin 8: salt spray is greatest near breaking waves and drops off rapidly in the first
  300 to 3,000 feet inland; exposed connectors and fasteners within 3,000 feet of the coast should be
  hot-dip galvanized or stainless steel; mechanically galvanized coatings are thinner and discouraged.
  https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/tb8-corrosion_protection_metal_connectors_coastal_areas.pdf
- FEMA P-2181 Fact Sheet 3.3.1 (sloped roofs): same 3,000-foot guidance for exposed roof connectors and
  fasteners. https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_p-2181-fact-sheet-3-3-1-roof-systems-sloped-roofs.pdf
