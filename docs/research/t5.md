## TDI catastrophe area (all four pages)
- Designated area = 14 first-tier coastal counties + parts of Harris County: La Porte, Morgan's Point,
  Pasadena, Seabrook, Shoreacres, inside city limits AND east of SH 146. TWIA FAQ
  https://www.twia.org/faqs/ ; TWIA coverage https://www.twia.org/coverage-eligibility/ ;
  TDI https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/generalquestio.html ; TDI adopted codes (designated areas unchanged
  with the April 2026 codes) https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/adopted-codes.html
- Seabrook: only the part east of SH 146 inside city limits. TDI order 96-1468
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/orders/documents/961468cat.pdf ; TDI 2026 TWIA building code rules
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/rules/documents/2026twiadiv6.pdf . Morgan's Point is entirely east of 146.
- Webster: NOT designated (not one of the five Harris County cities). TWIA eligibility requires the
  property be in the designated catastrophe area (TWIA). No WPI-8 for Webster.
- Friendswood: Galveston County part is in (first-tier county); Harris County part is not (Friendswood is
  not one of the five named Harris cities).
- League City: Galveston County (first-tier). Did not verify whether any small part is in Harris County,
  so the page says "Galveston County addresses, which covers League City" and does not claim a split.

## League City
- Permit list: re-roofing over 100 sq ft (1 square) requires a permit. https://www.leaguecitytx.gov/790/Permits
- Ordinance 2022-06 (Chapter 22): permits required before replacement of all residential roofs within city
  limits; permit holder must provide a copy of the TDI report certifying compliance; wood shingles/shakes
  not permitted on new residential structures (limited repair exceptions).
  https://leaguecitytx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/19653/...
- FAQ: work without a permit is an ordinance violation (code enforcement visit or fine); proof of permit
  often needed for insurance and resale. https://www.leaguecitytx.gov/FAQ.aspx?QID=63
- Online Permit Portal; Building Department 281-554-1429 (quick links page).
- Left out: third-party fee estimates (permitmint, permitplace): not official.

## Friendswood
- Residential re-roof/siding permit application asks square footage replaced and total cost/valuation;
  inspections: Galveston County = submit WPI-8, no city inspection; Harris County = city final inspection.
  https://www.ci.friendswood.tx.us/DocumentCenter/View/1861/... (form is several years old: page tells
  readers to confirm current process indirectly by asking the roofer/city).
- Residential building requirements: permits approved and issued BEFORE work starts; online permit portal.
  https://www.ci.friendswood.tx.us/DocumentCenter/View/303/...
- City posts monthly "Permits issued" reports including re-roof permits.
  https://www.ci.friendswood.tx.us/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/203
- Left out: the $90 re-roof fee seen in monthly reports (not a published fee schedule); the 910 S.
  Friendswood Dr. address (only from a search summary).

## Webster
- Building Division page; permits filed online through the permit portal; 281-338-2925 listed for online
  permit help. https://www.cityofwebster.com/232/Building-Division ;
  https://www.cityofwebster.com/m/newsflash/home/detail/429
- Contractor License Registration form: general contractors do not register; specialty contractors submit
  license, proof of insurance and business details; no permit becomes effective until the required license
  and certificate of insurance are on file; no registration fee.
  https://www.cityofwebster.com/FormCenter/Building-Department-14/Contractor-License-Registration-60
  (Whether a roofer counts as a "specialty contractor" in Webster was NOT confirmed; the page tells
  readers to ask.)
- Residential permit application exists (2019 form); no roofing-specific fee found, so no fee on the page.

## Seabrook
- Permit list includes new roofs and roof repairs; permitted work cannot start until issued.
  https://www.seabrooktx.gov/152/Permits
- Residential Construction: all structures must meet the city's adopted wind design requirement of 150 mph
  (ultimate); new construction plans sealed by a Texas structural engineer on the TDI list.
  https://www.seabrooktx.gov/153/Residential-Construction
- Permit application: starting without a permit may double permit fees; roof replacement listed under
  residential renovation/repair. https://www.seabrooktx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/220/Building-Permit-Application-PDF
- After Harvey: Storm Damage Recovery Permit; owners could remove damaged material before applying.
  https://seabrooktx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3603/...
- Public information request log shows requests for roof permit history and windstorm certifications.
  https://seabrooktx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/25892/Public-Information-Request-Log-as-of-9-30-26?bidId=
- Department of Community Development, 1700 First Street, 281-291-5669 (from city SOP documents).
