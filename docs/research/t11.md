## Shared windstorm facts (all three pages)
- From April 1, 2026, WPI-1 applications must be certified under the 2024 IRC or IBC; wind speed is set per
  structure by location; designated catastrophe areas unchanged; most of the area is in the windborne debris
  region. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/adopted-codes.html
- Legacy zone maps (2003/2006 codes): Seaward / Inland I / Inland II. Galveston County page lists Texas City in
  Inland I (with League City, Kemah, Dickinson). https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/maps/galvesto.html ; Brazoria
  page lists Alvin in Inland II (with Pearland, Manvel, Rosharon). https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/maps/brazoria.html
- Small repairs under 100 sq ft not involving decking exempt. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/need-windstorm-inspection.html
- TNMP serves Alvin and Texas City (and Friendswood, League City); report outages/lines 888-866-7456; treat
  downed lines as energized; 911 if immediate hazard. https://tnmp.com/customers/power-outages-hazards

## La Porte
- Incorporated Aug 10, 1892; oldest incorporated town in east Harris County; Sylvan Beach name 1898.
  https://www.laportetx.gov/589/History-of-La-Porte
- Re-roof permit required even for owners; correct shingle type critical; CSS portal; no official contractor
  registration/form/fee; COI naming the city; Inspections 281-470-5073, permits@laportetx.gov, 604 W. Fairmont
  Pkwy. laportetx.gov FAQ TID=32, TID=28; /1370/Contractor-Registration; FAQ QID=218
- Lomax: separate city consolidated after April 5, 1980 election; city FAQ places Lomax area west of SH 146 and
  north of Spencer Hwy. Brookglen: southwest, deed restrictions (2008 repetitive loss analysis, View/1345).
  Fairmont Park, Shady River: garbage service areas (/445).
- TDI Harris County page + TWIA FAQ: inside city limits and east of SH 146. TWIA manual: building official's
  statement preferred for La Porte for 1-1-88 to 3-1-96.
- Sylvan Beach pavilion: 1930s pavilion destroyed by tornado from 1943 hurricane; county bought park 1954;
  new hurricane-resistant pavilion opened 1956 (Harris County Historical Commission marker).
- Beryl: 97,000 cubic yards of debris, largest since Ike; 1,000+ cuts of leaning trees/limbs; CenterPoint;
  Sylvan Beach pier badly damaged. laportetx.gov CivicAlerts AID=325, 345; newsflash 371
- Ike: NHC TCR, highest high water mark 17.5 ft about 10 n mi inland in Chambers County.
- Oct 31 2015 tornado: Click2Houston, at least 70 La Porte homes damaged. Rating conflicting (EF1 vs 115 mph
  claim), so no rating or wind speed given.
