## Shared facts used on all four pages (group B)
Texas has no state roofing license, HB 3344 (2025) failed (RCAT); TDI: contractor may not waive/rebate/absorb a deductible,
$1,000+ insurance-paid contracts need the deductible notice, a contractor may not act as a public adjuster on a property it
works on (TDI "Roofing and insurance, know the law").
