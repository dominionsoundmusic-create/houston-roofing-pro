## Licensing (page 1)
- [S] HB 3344 (89th Leg., 2025) would have had TDLR license reroofing contractors. Left pending in committee at the
  April 15, 2025 hearing; reported favorably as substituted April 30; committee report sent to Calendars May 8, 2025;
  LegiScan status "died in chamber". Fiscal note: license requirement would have taken effect Sept 1, 2026.
  Author's statement in the bill analysis: Texas does not currently require a state-issued license for reroofing
  contractors. Sources: capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/analysis/pdf/HB03344H.pdf, .../summcomm/pdf/HB03344.pdf,
  .../fiscalnotes/html/HB03344H.htm, legiscan.com/TX/bill/HB3344/2025 (search result listed /2160; /2025 used as label URL,
  fact-checker should confirm the LegiScan URL).
- [S] 89th regular session adjourned sine die June 2, 2025 (sll.texas.gov spotlight).
- [S] RCAT program voluntary, run by the association; RCAT says TDLR does not license roofing contractors. Residential:
  $300,000 general liability or $100,000 bond; commercial $500,000. Exams: basic business and safety plus residential and/or
  commercial, 70 percent; one-year license, renew with 8 CE hours. rcat.net/licensing.html, /consumers.html, /advocacy.html.
  Left out: fees and years-in-business requirement (RCAT pages conflict: 2 vs 3 years).
- [S] TDI: private employers may choose workers' comp, not required in most cases; non-subscribers must notify employees
  and DWC. tdi.texas.gov/wc/employer/coverage.html, tdi.texas.gov/wc/nonsubscriber.html.
- [S] City of Houston: general contractors are not professionally licensed by the city or state (OBO New Business Guide PDF,
  houstontx.gov/obo/solutionscenter/guides/NEW_BUSINESS_GUIDE.pdf; Houston Permitting Center Residential Permitting 101).
- [G] City of Houston: roof covering up to 100 sq ft exempt; re-roof or overlay needs residential roofing permit (HPWCODE1140).
- [S] League City: contractors register with the Building Department; proof of insurance naming the city as certificate
  holder, minimum $250,000; annual registration fee $150 (not stated on page); re-roof over 100 sq ft needs a permit.
  leaguecitytx.gov/47/Building, leaguecitytx.gov/FAQ.aspx?QID=63, leaguecitytx.gov/4735 (fee schedule).
- [S] Missouri City: solicitation not permitted without the appropriate permit; no solicitation at residences before 9 am or
  after 9 pm. missouricitytx.gov/1172/Citizen-Information-on-Solicitors.
- NOT VERIFIED, left out: City of Houston peddler/solicitor license (only third-party sources); Sugar Land solicitor rules
  (third-party only); Friendswood rules (nothing found); SOSDirect fee.
- [S] Texas Comptroller Franchise Tax Account Status search: search by name, taxpayer number or SOS file number; shows
  right to transact business in Texas. comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/account-status/search.
- [S] Shingle maker system warranty (GAF System Plus brochure): contractor must register within 30 days of installation;
  warranty not in effect until homeowner receives written confirmation. Brand not named in copy; named in source URL only.
- [S] TDI know the law: illegal for contractor to offer to waive, rebate or absorb deductible; $1,000+ contracts paid with
  insurance proceeds need deductible notice; contractor may not act as public adjuster or advertise to adjust (Ins. Code
  4102.163); contractors may provide estimates and discuss them with the insurer/adjuster; report violations to the AG
  at 800-621-0508.
- [S] Texas AG: door-to-door sales over $25 away from seller's place of business can be cancelled within three business
  days, written notice required; never pay in full up front; consider credit card; do not sign completion papers or make
  final payment until satisfied; do not sign insurance check over to a contractor; complaints 800-621-0508.
  texasattorneygeneral.gov door-door-sales-3-day-right-rescission, disaster-scams, and the release
  "paxton-issues-consumer-alert-warning-texans-scammers-aftermath-houston-storms" (only its title seen; the page says only
  that the AG has warned about scammers after Houston storms, no date claimed).
- [W] Bus. & Com. Code Ch. 58: contractor without a physical address in the county or an adjacent county for at least a year
  generally may not require full or partial payment before starting disaster remediation work; contract notice required.
  TDI fraud-toolkit, Bulletin B-0006-21, statutes BC.58 (sister fact-check confirmed scope).
- Not used: number of roofing companies in Texas (no Census figure found). FAQ on that question not written.

## After storm damage (page 3)
- [G] derecho, Beryl, Community Impact 2.2 million customers without power, IBHS.
- [W] NHC Beryl tropical cyclone report URL (sister source).
- [W] TDI: insurer may deny a claim if you do not take reasonable steps to prevent further damage (home-damaged-faq);
  make temporary repairs, keep receipts, do not make permanent repairs before the adjuster sees the damage (recoverytips).
- [W] TDI Consumer Bill of Rights: acknowledge within 15 days; accept/deny within 15 business days after receiving
  requested info; 45-day extension with reason; weather catastrophe adds 15 days.
- [W] TDI and FEMA: standard home policies do not cover flooding.
- [G] TWIA wind and hail claims must be filed within one year (no TWIA URL seen by g2; not listed in sources).
