## Storm damage (/storm-damage-roof-repair/)
- Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. NOAA CPC: https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/outlooks/Background.html
- Harvey (Aug 2017): the NHC final figure was 51.88 in. at Cedar Bayou, the continental US record for one storm.
  FOX 26: https://www.fox26houston.com/news/final-harvey-rainfall-totals-peak-winds-from-national-hurricane-center .
  NWS Houston called it the worst flood in Houston's history. ABC13: https://abc7.com/2356341
- Derecho, May 16 2024: NWS HGX May 2024 summary says a swath of 100+ mph winds went through the metro.
  https://weather.gov/media/hgx/climate/summary/May_2024_Regional_Climate_Summary.pdf . Survey estimates of
  90 to 100 mph in Baytown, an EF-1 near Cypress, a major disaster declared for seven counties, and mostly
  straight-line wind damage: FOX Weather https://www.foxweather.com/weather-news/thunderstorm-damage-houston-texas.amp
- Beryl, July 8 2024: landfall near Matagorda as a Category 1 storm with 80 mph winds (NHC, reported by KHOU):
  https://khou.com/article/weather/weather-impact/where-did-hurricane-beryl-make-landfall/285-3ba122ec-be1f-4ee4-bf01-66263353771c .
  On the page I wrote "southwest of Houston" to avoid naming a place outside the service area. More than 2.2M
  customers without power: Community Impact (same source as /roof-repair/).
- Flood is excluded from standard home policies and needs a separate policy; most flood policies have a 30-day
  wait. TDI: https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/flood-insurance-cost.html . FloodSmart says coverage starts 30 days
  after purchase, with exceptions such as a loan closing: https://www.floodsmart.gov/policy-terms
- Inland policies probably cover wind. In the 14 coastal counties and certain parts of Harris County, policies
  likely exclude windstorm, so wind and hail coverage is bought from TWIA. TDI worksheet:
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/documents/howorksheet.pdf
- TWIA claims must be filed within one year (verified in WRITING-GUIDE).
- TDI scam warnings: a contract presented as an estimate or release; never sign one with blanks; do not pay in
  full up front; be wary of out-of-town references and door-to-door sales; deductible waivers are illegal;
  report them to the AG at 800-621-0508; pay by check or card. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/contractor-scams.html ,
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/storms/know-signs-of-contractor-scam.html
- After a declared disaster, contractors from outside the area may not ask for payment before work starts (TDI).
  The SB 985 (2019) analysis says Business and Commerce Code Ch. 58 covers contractors without a physical
  address in the county or an adjacent county for at least a year, with an escrow exception:
  https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/86R/analysis/html/SB00985I.htm . I did not state the penalty, because I only
  saw the bill analysis.
- City of Houston permit: the 100 sq ft exemption and the re-roof permit (verified in WRITING-GUIDE, HPWCODE1140).

## Hail (/hail-damage-roof-repair/)
- IBHS "Is it hail damage?" lists random impact marks, bruising (soft spots), cracks, ridge caps as especially
  vulnerable, granule loss alone not proving hail, the look-alikes (edge wear, aging, defects, heavy rain), and
  collateral dents on A/C units, gutters and flashing: https://ibhs.org/is-it-hail-damage/
- GAF TAB-R-108 says the mat can fracture without being immediately visible, and hidden damage can turn into
  cracks and tears later.
- IBHS 2025 study (Frontiers in Materials): hail under 1 in. reduces resistance to later large hail.
  Weathered shingles hit by small hail were about 10x more susceptible. Insurance Journal, Oct 2 2025:
  https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2025/10/02/842108.htm
- Test squares: 10 by 10 ft (100 sq ft) on each slope, placed away from tree cover, counting the hits. The
  method comes from Haag research and is taught to home inspectors by InterNACHI:
  https://www.nachi.org/hail-damage-part12-39.htm . Thresholds vary by carrier, so the page gives no hit
  count. The critique that a square is only a sample is from Claims Journal (May 2026):
  https://amp.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/05/06/337211.htm
- UL 2218 sorts shingles into Class 1 to 4. GAF says Class 4 survives a 2 in. steel ball dropped from 20 ft:
  https://www.gaf.com/en-us/roofing-materials/residential-roofing-materials/shingles/class-4-shingles
  (named only as product information).
- TDI says each insurer sets its own impact-resistant credit and Class 4 gets the largest. The PC068 form is
  filled out by the contractor, and TWIA needs the product labeled:
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/company/roofing-discounts.html . TDI Bulletin B-0008-26 (2026) encourages
  insurers to offer supported discounts.
- Texas Property Code 202.011 limits HOA bans on wind- and hail-resistant shingles:
  https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_202.011
- Hail events, NWS HGX Storm Data PDFs:
  - Apr 2000: hail broke windows and damaged cars in Province Square, Katy (apr00hgx.pdf)
  - Apr 2003: 1.75 in. in Baytown, Mont Belvieu, South Houston and League City (apr03hgx.pdf)
  - May 2006: 3.00 in. near Hooks Airport and 1.75 in. in Jersey Village (may06hgx.pdf)
  - Oct 22 2008: 1.75 in. near Beltway 8 and the Hardy Toll Road (oct08hgx.pdf)
  - Apr 5 2023: 1.50 in. near Falcon Landing Blvd, plus 1.00 in. at Pecan Grove and 1.2 mi west of Katy
    (apr23hgx.pdf; the date comes from matching StormerSite and HGX entries)
- Insurance Code 4102.163: a contractor may not act as a public adjuster (verified in WRITING-GUIDE).
- Prices: none given. The page lists what drives price and points to the cost guide.
