Sugar Land checks the roof. Make sure your roofer knows it.

Sugar Land is one of the few places around Houston where a roof replacement is a permitted, inspected job. The city's own homeowner guide lists roof repair and roof replacement among the common projects that need a permit, and a permit stays good for 180 days. That is a real protection for you, because someone other than the roofer looks at the work. It also sorts Sugar Land roofing companies quickly. The ones that work here every week pull the permit as a matter of course. The ones passing through after a storm sometimes hope you will not ask.

So ask. Before you sign, get it in writing that the roofing company will pull the city permit in its name and schedule the inspections. If a roofer suggests skipping it to save time, that tells you how the rest of the job will go.

Roofs permitted this year go on under a newer code

Sugar Land moved to the 2024 International Codes for permit applications submitted on or after January 21, 2026. Applications filed before that date are still reviewed under the 2021 codes. For a homeowner, the practical point is simple: your roofer's permit application will be checked against the code in force the day it is filed, so a crew that learned its habits in unincorporated county work, where there is no inspection, needs to be up to date here.

Greatwood and New Territory: Sugar Land now, permits too

Greatwood and New Territory carried Sugar Land mailing addresses for decades while sitting outside the city in unincorporated Fort Bend County. That changed on December 12, 2017, when the city annexed both communities and took over permitting and inspections there, along with water, police and fire. The HOAs stayed in place, since annexation did not touch them.

That timing matters for roofs. Both communities opened in 1989, and many homes are now on their second roof or due for it. The first replacement may have gone on with no permit at all, back when the county had no inspection. The next one needs a city permit and an HOA approval, both. Owners there sometimes hear otherwise from a roofer who remembers how it used to work.

Two approvals, in order: in First Colony, Telfair, Greatwood, New Territory, Riverstone and most other Sugar Land neighborhoods, the HOA approves the shingle color and style, and the city approves the work. Get the HOA letter first so the permit application does not stall.

What Sugar Land roofs face

The Brazos River bottom is flat and open, so wind reaches roofs with little to slow it down. Hurricane Beryl in July 2024 came ashore at Matagorda and tracked north across Fort Bend County, lifting shingle tabs and breaking seal strips across the city. Summer heat bakes the granules off older shingles, and granules in the gutters are one of the plainest signs a roof is near the end. Most original roofs in the neighborhoods built through the 1990s and 2000s have already been replaced once; many of the second roofs are now at fifteen years or more.

How to compare Sugar Land roofing companies

  • Permit in their name. Written into the contract, with the inspections they will schedule.
  • Proof of insurance. A certificate of liability and workers' compensation coverage, sent to you directly.
  • Addresses in Fort Bend County. Roofs they did here in the last year or two that you can drive past.
  • A scope you can read. Tear-off, underlayment, drip edge, flashing, pipe boots, ventilation and a price per sheet for decking.
  • No deductible games. Texas law requires you to pay your deductible, and it is illegal for a roofer to offer to waive or absorb it.

Common questions

Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Sugar Land?

Yes. The City of Sugar Land lists roof repair and roof replacement among the common home projects that need a permit, and a permit is good for 180 days. Your roofing company should pull it in its own name.

Do Greatwood and New Territory need city roofing permits?

Yes. Both communities were annexed into the City of Sugar Land on December 12, 2017, and the city handles permits and inspections there now. The HOAs still approve colors and styles separately.

Which building code applies to a new roof in Sugar Land?

Permit applications submitted on or after January 21, 2026 are reviewed under the 2024 International Codes. Applications filed before that date stay under the 2021 codes.

Are roofers licensed in Texas?

No. Texas has no state license for roofing contractors, which is why the city permit, proof of insurance and local references matter so much when choosing one.
