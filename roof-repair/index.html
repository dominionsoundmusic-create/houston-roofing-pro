Houston roofs fail differently

A Dallas roof mostly fights hail. A Houston roof fights water and wind. The Gulf Coast humidity means the underside of the deck rarely dries out completely in summer, tropical downpours test every seam, and the region has taken two brutal wind events in quick succession: the May 2024 derecho that blew out windows downtown, and Hurricane Beryl that July, which left millions of CenterPoint customers without power and peeled shingles off roofs across the metro. Most Houston roof repairs trace back to one of those forces finding a weak spot.

The repairs that come up most

  • Wind-lifted shingles. Tropical storms and summer squalls break the seal strips. A shingle that has lifted once will lift again, and the next gust takes it. See wind damage.
  • Flashing. Where the roof meets a wall, chimney or dormer. In Houston's sideways rain, a small gap in flashing leaks more than a missing shingle in the middle of a slope. See flashing repair.
  • Pipe boots and vents. The rubber cracks in the heat, and the leak often shows up as a stain in a bathroom ceiling.
  • Soft decking. Water that gets in and stays, in this humidity, rots plywood faster than it would in a drier city. A repair that finds soft decking should replace that section, not shingle over it.
  • Tree damage. Houston's live oaks and pines drop limbs in every big storm.

Black streaks are not a repair. The dark streaking common on Houston roofs is algae. It is cosmetic, it does not mean the roof is failing, and a contractor who uses it to sell a replacement is worth a second opinion.

Repair, or is the roof finished?

One leak, one damaged area or a few lost shingles is a repair on almost any roof. If the shingles are brittle, curling and shedding granules, or leaks are turning up in several rooms, read the signs on the roof replacement page before paying for patch after patch.

On the coast: windstorm rules

If you live in Galveston County or another coastal area where windstorm coverage comes through the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association, roof work may need to be inspected and certified to the windstorm building code to keep that coverage in place. Tell the roofer where you are before work starts, and ask whether they handle the certification.

Hiring well

Texas does not license roofers, so ask for proof of insurance, a local address and references. Get the scope and price in writing. After a big storm, be careful with door-to-door crews and anyone asking for a large payment upfront. Texas law does not allow a contractor to pay, waive or rebate your insurance deductible.

Common questions

Is a roof leak covered by insurance in Houston?

Leaks from sudden damage such as wind, hail or a falling limb are usually covered, subject to your deductible. Leaks from age and wear usually are not. Coastal homes may have windstorm coverage through a separate policy.

How fast does a Houston roof leak cause damage?

Quickly. In Gulf Coast humidity, wet insulation and decking can start growing mold within days, so even a slow leak is worth fixing promptly.

Do black streaks mean I need a new roof?

No. They are algae and are cosmetic. They can be soft washed off, but they are not a reason to replace a sound roof.

Can a roof be repaired during hurricane season?

Yes. Repairs go ahead between storms, and emergency tarping can be done after one. It is worth fixing weak spots before a storm arrives rather than after.
