Roof Repair in Katy, TX

Katy is a young suburb built fast, which produces two roofing situations you do not get in older parts of Houston: whole streets that need roofs in the same year, and homes carrying damage from flooding that has nothing to do with water landing on the roof.

Whole streets age out together

Master-planned Katy went up in waves. Cinco Ranch, Seven Meadows, Firethorne, Cross Creek Ranch — each phase built in a couple of years, by the same crews, with the same materials.

That means the roofs fail together. If three houses on your street have had roofs done this year, yours is the same age and the same product. It is not a coincidence and it is not bad luck.

This is useful information rather than bad news. Ask a neighbour who has already done it what they paid, who they used, and what the contractor found underneath. On a street of identical houses that intelligence is unusually reliable.

Builder-grade shingles and what they actually give you

Production builders fit three-tab shingles rated for twenty to twenty-five years. That rating assumes a moderate climate.

In Houston heat, with the ultraviolet load and the daily expansion and contraction, fifteen to eighteen years is a more realistic expectation — and less than that on a poorly ventilated attic, which is common in tightly built subdivisions.

So a 2008 build is due now. A 2015 build has a few years left. Neither is a defect; it is what that product does in this climate.

The flooding connection nobody makes

Katy properties near the Barker and Addicks reservoirs have flood history, and water on the ground does not damage a roof. What happens afterwards sometimes does.

  • Attic access opened repeatedly during remediation, with people and equipment moving through a space that was never designed for traffic.
  • Insulation removed, which changes attic temperature and moisture behaviour and can shorten shingle life from beneath.
  • Ductwork and equipment replaced, with new penetrations cut and old ones patched.
  • Decking walked on by trades who are not roofers.

None of it shows immediately. It shows two or three years later as a leak at a penetration, and nobody connects it to the flood.

Check the warranty before you pay anybody

This is worth ten minutes before a repair on a newer Katy home.

WarrantyTypical span
Builder workmanshipOften one to two years — usually expired
Builder structuralFrequently ten years, and may cover decking and framing
Shingle manufacturerTwenty to thirty years on the product itself, transferable in some cases
Previous roofer's labour warrantyWhatever the paperwork says, and only as good as the company

A manufacturer warranty covers the shingle, not the installation, and the overwhelming majority of failures are installation. But if shingles are failing early and uniformly across a whole street, a manufacturing claim is worth exploring.

Before you call

  • Year the house was built, and whether the roof is original.
  • Whether neighbours have recently replaced theirs.
  • Whether the property has ever flooded, and whether attic work was done.
  • Whether the problem is one spot or spread across the roof.
