Houston Expert Roofers ("the line", "we", "us") is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group. It runs the website houstonexpertroofers.com and a phone line that connects callers with independent, locally owned roofing companies across greater Houston; it does no roofing work itself. This policy explains what information the line collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have.

Information collected

  • Phone calls. When you call, an automated assistant asks which service you need and briefly what is going on. The call record (your phone number, the time of the call and what you told the assistant) is kept so the call can be routed and, if the roofing company cannot answer, so your name, callback number, city, need and urgency can be passed along for a call back.
  • The contact form on /contact/ collects your name, phone number, city or ZIP code, what you need and any message you add.
  • The storm claim checklist form on /hail-claim-checklist/ collects your first name, email address, ZIP code and, only if you choose, a phone number.
  • Automatically. Like most websites, the site and its host log technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and the referring site.

How information is used

  • To route your call or request to an independent roofing company that takes calls in your area, so it can contact you.
  • To send you the checklist you asked for.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used and to keep it working.
  • To meet legal obligations and to protect the line's legal rights.

How information is shared

Your information is not sold. When you call or send a request, your name, phone number, location and the details of your roofing need are shared with the independent roofing company the line connects you with, so it can contact you. That company is a separate business with its own privacy practices. Information is otherwise shared only with service providers that help run the line and the site, such as telephone, form and website hosting (Netlify) and analytics providers, and only as far as they need it to do that work, or where the law requires it.

Cookies, analytics and translation

The site uses Google Analytics to measure visits and clicks on the phone number, which sets cookies in your browser. If you choose Español in the header, the site loads Google Translate and sets a cookie that remembers your language. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings; the site still works without them, although the language switch may not stay set.

Text messages

The line does not send marketing text messages. A roofing company you are connected with may contact you by phone or text about your request under its own policies.

Retention and security

Records are kept only as long as needed to route requests, keep basic business records and meet legal requirements. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information the line holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, subject to records the law requires to be kept. Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. To make a request, call {{ site.business.phone_display }} or use the contact form.

Children

The site is meant for adults arranging work on a property. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. This page is a plain description of the line's practices, not legal advice.
