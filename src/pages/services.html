One number for every kind of roof job

Roofing services in Houston fall into a few groups: fixing a problem, dealing with storm damage, putting on a new roof, and the specialty work on metal, flat and commercial roofs.

You do not have to know which group yours is in. Call, describe what you see, and the automated assistant passes your call to an independent, locally owned roofing contractor in greater Houston. That contractor inspects the roof and tells you whether it is a repair, a claim or a replacement, with its own written estimate.

The pages below are written to help you ask better questions, whoever you hire. None of them is a quote, and the line never prices or performs any work.

Repairs and leaks

Most calls start here: one weak spot on a roof that is otherwise sound. A careful repair finds the source, which is often uphill from the stain, and fixes the cause rather than smearing cement over it.

Storm, hail and wind damage

Storm damage is usually an insurance question as well as a roofing one. You file the claim; the roofer documents the damage and can meet the adjuster on the roof. Texas law requires you to pay your own deductible.

An inspection tells you where the roof stands. A replacement is the biggest roofing purchase most homeowners make, so it pays to know what the quote should list before you compare any.

Flat and commercial roofs

Low-slope roofs on offices, warehouses, strip centers, garages and patio covers use membranes and coatings rather than shingles, and they fail in different ways, mostly at seams, drains and standing water.
