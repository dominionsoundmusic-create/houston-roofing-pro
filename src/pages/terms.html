What the line does

Houston Expert Roofers is a referral service. A phone call is picked up by an automated assistant, which either connects you with an independent, locally owned roofing company that serves your area or gives that company your details for a return call. Requests sent through a form go to such a company, which then phones you. The line itself never does roofing work, inspects roofs, prepares estimates, sells materials, files or manages insurance claims, or sends workers to your home.

Your contract is with the roofer you choose

Each roofing company the line connects you with runs its own independent business. That company decides its prices, looks at the job, writes any estimate, sets the schedule, does the work and answers for it. Warranties, payment arrangements and cancellation rules are matters between you and the roofer, and you should get them in writing. Houston Expert Roofers is not part of that contract and makes no promise about any company's licensing, insurance, quality of work or availability.

Cost of using the line

Calling and being connected cost you nothing. Roofing companies may pay the line a referral fee.

Licensing, insurance and claims

Texas does not issue a state license for roofing contractors. Before work begins, ask any roofer for a certificate of insurance, a written contract and references, and confirm whether your city requires a permit or registration. Insurance claims are between you and your insurer. Texas law requires you to pay your own deductible, and a contractor may not act as your public adjuster.

Information you give

Whatever you share by phone or form (your name, phone number, location and what the roof needs, for example) goes to the roofing company taking your request, which uses it to reach you. The privacy policy has the details.
