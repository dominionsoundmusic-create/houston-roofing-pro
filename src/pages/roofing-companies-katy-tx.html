Katy roofing companies work in two very different places

Ask ten Katy homeowners where they live and you will hear one town name for two quite different addresses. One is the City of Katy, a small municipality with its own permit office, its own building code adoption and its own list of registered contractors. The other is the far larger Katy area that grew up around it, where master-planned communities like Cinco Ranch spread south of Interstate 10 into Fort Bend and Harris counties.

That split decides the paperwork on your roof before a single shingle comes off. Inside the city limits the roofer deals with city permits and city registration. Outside them, the rules come from wherever your house actually sits, and your homeowners association usually has a say on what the new roof looks like. A roofing company that works Katy every week should be able to tell you which side of the line you are on without guessing.

City of Katy permits and the roofer registration

The City of Katy's Permits and Building Services division says permits are required for all new construction, remodeling or roofing, and that the city has adopted the 2021 International Building Codes. A replacement roof inside the city limits is roofing work, so it goes through the city.

The city also runs a contractor registration, and roofing is one of the trades on its form. According to the city, registration costs $200 and stays valid for 12 months from payment. The form asks for a general liability insurance certificate of at least $300,000 that names the City of Katy as certificate holder, and it notes that a contractor who has never worked in the city may need a pre-construction meeting. For a homeowner, that registration is one of the few local records you can actually ask to see.

The Permits Office is at 901 Avenue C, Katy, TX 77493, phone 281-391-4830. Call it if you want to confirm what your job needs or whether a roofer is registered.

Outside the city limits

A Katy mailing address does not put you inside the City of Katy. Cinco Ranch, for one, is a census-designated place, not a city, so the city's permit and registration rules do not reach it. If your property tax statement does not list the City of Katy as a taxing unit, you are probably outside it. Ask the roofer to name, in the contract, any permit it will pull for your address and from which office.

Questions to ask a roofer in Katy

Any honest roofing company that works Katy can answer these quickly. Write the answers down, or better, ask for them in the estimate.

Paperwork that is specific to Katy

  • Is my address inside the City of Katy, and if so, will you pull the roofing permit in your name?
  • Are you currently registered with the City of Katy, and can I see it?
  • Will you prepare the HOA submittal, and which approved shingle colors does my subdivision allow?
  • Can you send your certificate of liability insurance directly from your insurer?

The roof itself

  • After the 2023 and 2024 storms, did you look for hail bruising and broken seal strips, and can I see the photos?
  • Will you tear off to the deck, and what is your price per sheet for any rotten decking?
  • Are new drip edge, pipe boots, flashing and ridge vent in the price, or reused?
  • What is your workmanship warranty, in writing, and will you register the shingle warranty?
