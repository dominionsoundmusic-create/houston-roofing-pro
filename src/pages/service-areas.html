How coverage works across greater Houston

Searching for roofing near Houston turns up companies from all over the region. The line works the other way round: you call one number, say where you are, and the call goes to an independent roofing company that takes jobs in your area.

There is no office in any of these towns, no crew and no truck. Houston Expert Roofers answers the phone and passes the call on. The roofing company you reach decides whether it can come out and when, and it quotes and does the work.

The paperwork is the part that differs between towns. Some cities permit and inspect a re-roof, some only when the decking is replaced, and unincorporated county areas have no city permit office at all, so the county's rules apply. Along the coast, the state windstorm program adds an inspection of its own. Each town page below explains its own rules, with sources.

Harris County

The City of Houston, including Kingwood, permits re-roofs. Around it are smaller cities with their own rules and large unincorporated communities such as Cypress, Spring, Atascocita and Channelview, where there is no city permit office and the Harris County Engineer lists no separate re-roof permit.

Montgomery County

North of Houston the land turns to pine forest. Trees shade and soak roofs, drop needles into valleys and bring limbs down in storms. The Woodlands runs on covenants rather than city permits.

Brazoria, Galveston and Chambers counties: the windstorm towns

These counties are in the Texas Department of Insurance's designated catastrophe area, along with the bayside parts of five Harris County cities east of State Highway 146. Pearland, Friendswood and Baytown cross county lines, and only their parts in Brazoria, Galveston or Chambers County are in the catastrophe area; Baytown is not on the state's list of Harris County windstorm cities. A roofer working in the catastrophe area must build most roof work to the windstorm code and have it inspected while it is visible.

What to ask, wherever you live

  • Is my address inside city limits, and does this job need a permit? Will you pull it in your name?
  • Is my home in the windstorm catastrophe area, and will you schedule the inspection and get me the WPI-8?
  • Does my HOA or deed restriction need to approve the shingle and color first?
  • Can you send me your certificate of liability insurance and a few addresses of roofs near me?
