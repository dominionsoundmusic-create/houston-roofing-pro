What Houston Expert Roofers is

Houston Expert Roofers is two things: a phone number that answers around the clock, and the roofing guides on this website. It is owned by Dominion Digital Group and operates as a d/b/a under that company.

The phone line answers with an automated assistant, finds out what is going on with your roof and where you live, and transfers the call to an independent, locally owned roofing company that takes work in that part of greater Houston. If that company cannot pick up, your details go to it for a call back. That is the whole service.

The name is the brand of the line, not a claim about any roofer's skill. The roofing companies are separate businesses. They inspect, price and do the work, and you pay them, not the line.

Why the site teaches you to check the roofer

Texas does not license roofing contractors, and every big Houston storm brings a wave of crews that leave when the claims run out. The line cannot vouch for anyone, so the guides give you the questions instead.

Plain answers

Each service page explains what usually goes wrong on Houston roofs and what a careful repair or replacement should include, so you can follow what the roofer tells you.

Local rules

Town pages cover permits, homeowner association approvals and the coastal windstorm program, which change from one city line to the next.

Sources you can read

Facts come from public sources such as the Texas Department of Insurance, city permit offices and the National Weather Service, listed at the bottom of each page.

How the line is paid

Calling is free for you, always. Roofing companies may pay the line a referral fee for calls it connects. That fee is the line's only income from roofing calls, and it never appears on anything you pay.

Because a company may pay for the referral, you should treat its estimate like any other: read it line by line, ask what each item means, and compare it with another quote if you want one. Nothing about the call obliges you to hire the company.

What you will not find here

No reviews, ratings, star counts, years in business, team photos, license numbers or promises about how fast someone will arrive. The line has none of those to offer honestly, so it does not claim them.
