A railroad town on the coastal prairie

Roofing in Alvin, TX means working on flat, open prairie in northern Brazoria County, where wind crosses miles of pasture before it reaches a roof. The town grew from a Santa Fe Railroad flag station near the head of Mustang Slough; the Texas State Historical Association records the first house there in 1879 and the name Alvin from 1881.

Today Alvin is a mix of an older downtown, established streets around it and newer subdivisions such as Mustang Crossing, Mustang Ridge, Fairway Lakes and Bayou Point that the city has listed among its residential developments, with country homes and acreage on the roads out of town. A roof on a farmhouse with a metal roof and a roof on a new two story in a subdivision raise different questions, but they share the same county rules.

Inland, but still inside the coastal windstorm area

Many Alvin owners are surprised to learn their roof falls under the same state program as a beach house. The reason is the county line: the Texas Department of Insurance's designated catastrophe area includes all of Brazoria County. Here is how the rules have shifted over time for a town like Alvin.

PeriodHow the wind requirement was setWhere Alvin stood
Under the 2006 code era mapsThree zones by distance from the coast: Seaward, Inland I and Inland IITDI's Brazoria County page lists Alvin in Inland II, the farthest inland zone, with Pearland, Manvel and Rosharon

Either periodA roof replacement is inspected while it is installed, and TDI issues a WPI-8 certificate of compliance when the work passes

So an older note from a neighbor about "the inland zone" may no longer match how a new roof is designed. TDI also says most of the designated area now sits inside the windborne debris region. Ask the roofer which code edition and wind speed it is building your roof to, and which products and fastening meet it.

City of Alvin permits and contractor papers

The City of Alvin handles building permits through its Engineering Department, and its building permit application covers repairs as well as new work, with fees set by the city's Comprehensive Fee Ordinance in Chapter 28 of the city code. The same application states that the city does not perform energy or windstorm inspections, which is why the windstorm side of a re-roof runs through TDI rather than city hall.

Three local rules are worth knowing before a roof contract:

  • Contractor paperwork. The city's contractor form asks for copies of applicable state licenses, a driver's license and proof of general liability insurance naming the City of Alvin as certificate holder.
  • No wood shakes by default. Ordinance 19-F(a), amending the city's building chapter prohibits wood shingles and shakes in new construction or reroofing unless specifically authorized.
  • Torch work. The Fire Marshal's contractor packet says roofing that uses heat or other ignition sources must be done by a contractor licensed and bonded for that process, with a fire extinguisher kept on the roof. That matters on low slope roofs where torch applied membranes are an option.

Whether your particular roof job needs a city permit, and what it costs, is a question for the Engineering Department, which the permit application lists at 281-388-4271. The city's permits page says permits and inspections are required for new construction, remodeling and repairs, and lists the 2024 International codes with a 140 mph design wind speed per the code table. Codes change, so confirm before work starts.

Because Alvin does not feel coastal, a crew from outside the area may skip the state inspection. Missing it can leave you without a WPI-8 when an insurer or buyer asks. Raise it on the first call with the company you are put through to.

Farmhouses, barns and metal roofs

Acreage outside town brings metal roofs on houses and outbuildings, with screws, washers and panel laps that loosen over time. A company that takes Alvin calls can tell you whether it handles metal as well as shingles.

Alvin weather on the record

  1. July 1979, Tropical Storm Claudette. The National Weather Service Houston/Galveston office's hurricane guide records about 45 inches of rain at Alvin, and 43 inches in 24 hours on a private rain gauge a little north of Alvin, a total still cited by the National Weather Service as a continental United States record. Rain on that scale tests every valley, boot and flashing joint on a roof.
  2. August 2017, Hurricane Harvey. The National Hurricane Center reported record water levels on the Brazos and San Bernard rivers in Brazoria County and widespread flooding. In Alvin, a city capital projects report describes repairs to the banks of Mustang Bayou where the slope failed from Harvey's flood waters.
  3. July 8, 2024, Hurricane Beryl. The storm came ashore near Matagorda, and the National Weather Service Houston/Galveston office measured its highest gust in the region, 97 mph, near the Brazos River in Brazoria County.

After a storm, the power company to call in Alvin is Texas-New Mexico Power, whose Gulf Coast service list includes Alvin. Treat every downed line as live, report it to TNMP at 888-866-7456, and call 911 if anyone is in danger.

What to ask an Alvin roofer before you sign

Rules and records

  • Does this job need a City of Alvin permit, and will you apply for it?
  • Who notifies TDI, who schedules the windstorm inspections, and when will I receive the WPI-8?
  • Which code edition and wind speed are you designing to under the rules in force since April 1, 2026?
  • If I am in a subdivision with an HOA, who submits the roof for approval?

Materials and money

  • Which shingles or metal panels will you use, and what wind rating do they carry?
  • What will you do if the decking is soft, and what is the price per sheet?
  • Can you email me your certificate of liability insurance?
  • What does your workmanship warranty cover, and for how long, in writing?

Texas Property Code section 202.011 generally keeps a property owners' association from banning wind and hail resistant or energy efficient shingles that look like the shingles it already permits, though it can still require approval first.
