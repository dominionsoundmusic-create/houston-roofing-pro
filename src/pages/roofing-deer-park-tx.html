Outside the windstorm area, next door to it

The Texas Department of Insurance designates a coastal catastrophe area where roof work must meet a windstorm building code and be inspected as it goes on. In greater Houston that covers Brazoria, Chambers and Galveston counties plus five Harris County communities: La Porte, Morgan's Point, Seabrook, Shoreacres and Pasadena east of State Highway 146. Deer Park is not one of them.

For a Deer Park homeowner that means three practical things:

  • No WPI-8 to chase. A repair or a new roof does not need a TDI windstorm inspection or certificate of compliance.
  • Wind is a homeowners claim. Storm damage goes through your regular homeowners insurer, not the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association, which writes policies only in the designated area.
  • Know your deductible. Ask your agent whether your policy has a separate wind and hail deductible, and how it is calculated, before a storm rather than after.

TDI's maps are general guides, and the agency asks owners to verify an address if they are unsure. Its Windstorm Inspections office is at 1-800-248-6032 (option 4).

Storms that left repairs behind

Two storms in 2024 explain many of the repairs east Harris County roofs still need. On May 16, the National Weather Service office in League City described a derecho swath of winds over 100 mph through the Houston metro. On July 8, Hurricane Beryl made landfall as a Category 1 storm and knocked out power to more than two million CenterPoint Energy customers. Earlier, Hurricane Harvey in 2017 brought days of rain that the Harris County Flood Control District ties to roughly 120,000 flooded structures across the region, the kind of soaking that finds every tired boot and seam.

A shingle that lost its seal in one of those storms can drop back into place and look normal from the yard. It lifts again in the next gust and lets water run underneath. If your roof has not had a close look since 2024, a ceiling stain or granules in the gutters are reasons to call before the next storm season.
