Thanks. Here is what happens next.

A local roofing company has your name, number, city and what you need. It will call you to arrange a look at the roof.

  • Ask whether there is a charge for the visit before it is booked; many roofers inspect at no charge, but policies differ.
  • Ask for photos of anything the roofer finds and a written, itemized estimate.
  • You are free to get other quotes before deciding.
