Two printed pages for the days after a storm

After hail, wind or a tropical storm, the first few days decide a lot: what gets photographed, whether more water gets in, which contractor you trust, and whether the claim is filed in time.

The checklist puts all of that on two pages you can print and keep in a drawer before the next storm season. Fill in the form and it opens straight away.

What the checklist covers

  1. Right after the storm. Power line safety, staying off the roof, and writing down the date, time and hail size.
  2. Documenting the damage. The photos that matter, including gutters, vents, air conditioner fins and screens that show hail size.
  3. Stopping more damage. Buckets, moving belongings, having a roofer tarp an open roof, and keeping every receipt.
  4. Your policy. Percentage deductibles, replacement cost versus actual cash value, and reporting deadlines.
  5. Filing and the adjuster's visit. Why it helps to have the roofer on the roof when the adjuster comes.
  6. Hiring safely. Insurance certificates, a written scope, deposits, and the Texas rule against waiving deductibles.
  7. After the work. Invoices, permits, warranties and asking the insurer to release any held-back depreciation.

Print it before the next storm

Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, and Houston's spring thunderstorms bring hail and straight-line wind of their own. When a storm hits a whole region, roofers book up quickly and door-knockers arrive within days. A list you read in advance makes those days calmer.
