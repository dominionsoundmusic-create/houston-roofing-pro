The small city with a big address

The City of Richmond is the Fort Bend County seat, a small historic town on the Brazos River of about twelve thousand people. The Richmond mailing address reaches much further: Pecan Grove, Aliana, Harvest Green, Long Meadow Farms, Lakemont and the master-planned communities spreading west along the Grand Parkway and FM 359. Almost all of that is unincorporated Fort Bend County.

The difference shows up on roofing day. Inside the City of Richmond, building work goes through the city's permit office. In unincorporated Fort Bend County there is no building permit for an ordinary shingle replacement, so no one from the county inspects the work. Your HOA's architectural committee and your contract are the only checks, which makes the choice of roofer everything.

Two kinds of Richmond roof

Pecan Grove and the older neighborhoods. Pecan Grove went up from the late 1970s onward around its golf course and old pecan trees. Those houses are on their second or third roof, often under big trees. Expect questions about decking, old layers and flashing around chimneys and skylights.

Aliana, Harvest Green and the newer communities. These are less than fifteen years old and many are on original builder-grade roofs. Wind is their main enemy: the open, newly planted lots give it nothing to break against. A young roof that lifts or leaks may be a warranty or installation issue before it is a replacement, so check the builder's and shingle manufacturer's warranties first.

HOA approval is the step people forget. Nearly every Richmond-area community has deed restrictions on shingle color and style. Get the architectural approval in writing before the roofer orders material.

Weather along the Brazos

Hurricane Beryl came ashore at Matagorda in July 2024 and tracked north across Fort Bend County, lifting shingles and dropping trees throughout Richmond and Rosenberg. Hail comes in with spring storms across the coastal prairie. The Brazos itself is a flood risk more than a roof risk, but homes near it see the same heavy rain that tests every valley and pipe boot.

Choosing a roofing company in Richmond

  • They know whether you are in the city and pull a permit if you are.
  • They handle the HOA submittal or tell you exactly what it needs.
  • Insurance certificate, sent to you. Texas has no state roofing license.
  • Photos of the bare deck before the underlayment goes down, since no county inspector will see it.
  • The deductible stays with you. Texas law requires you to pay it.

Common questions

Do I need a roofing permit in Richmond, TX?

Only inside the City of Richmond, which runs its own permit office. Most Richmond addresses, including Pecan Grove, Aliana and Harvest Green, are unincorporated Fort Bend County, where an ordinary shingle replacement needs no building permit.

My Richmond house is new and the roof is already failing. What first?

Check the builder's warranty and the shingle manufacturer's warranty, then call your insurer if a storm caused it. A young roof that fails early may be an installation problem.

Does my HOA need to approve a new roof?

Almost certainly. Most Richmond-area communities restrict shingle color and style. Texas Property Code section 202.011 does stop an HOA from banning impact-resistant shingles that otherwise look like the approved ones.

Are roofers licensed in Texas?

No. There is no state roofing license, so ask for insurance, local references and a detailed written contract.
