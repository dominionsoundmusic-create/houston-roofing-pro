When a roof is actually finished

Age alone does not decide it. A Houston roof takes more ultraviolet and more heat cycling than the same roof in a milder climate, so a shingle rated for twenty-five years often gives fifteen to twenty here. But the signs matter more than the number.

  • Granules in the gutters, consistently. Some loss is normal. A steady accumulation means the shingles are shedding their protection.
  • Shingles curling or cupping at the edges. The mat has dried out and is pulling away.
  • Multiple leaks in different places. One leak is a repair. Three separate ones in a season is a roof telling you something.
  • Daylight visible in the attic anywhere other than a vent.
  • Sagging between rafters — decking that has absorbed water and lost strength.
  • A second layer already on there. Most codes allow two layers maximum, so a third is not an option and tear-off is coming regardless.

Three or more of those, and you are replacing rather than repairing. One or two, and a good contractor should be telling you a repair will hold. A contractor who recommends full replacement for a single leak is worth a second opinion.

The two warranties are not the same thing

This is where homeowners get caught. A shingle manufacturer's thirty-year warranty covers manufacturing defects in the shingle. It does not cover installation, and the overwhelming majority of roof failures are installation, not product.

The labour warranty is what protects you, it comes from the contractor, and it is worth exactly what that contractor is worth in five years. Ask how long they have been trading under that name at that address.

Timing, and why it matters here

If the choice is yours, replace outside storm season and outside the weeks immediately after a major hail event. Prices rise, crews are booked, and the out-of-state operators arrive.

Heat is a genuine constraint too. Shingles are installed with a self-sealing strip that needs warmth to bond, which Houston has plenty of — but extreme heat makes shingles soft enough to scuff under foot traffic. Spring and autumn are the comfortable windows for quality of work as well as for scheduling.
