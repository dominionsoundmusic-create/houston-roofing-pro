WHAT THE BUSINESS ACTUALLY IS (read this twice):
Houston Expert Roofers is a FREE PHONE LINE and website, NOT a roofing company. Nobody here climbs
on roofs, inspects, sells shingles, quotes, repairs, replaces, tarps, files claims or schedules
anything. When someone calls:
- An automated assistant answers, any hour, and asks which service they need (the same number
  serves roofing, air conditioning and heating, pressure washing and water damage) and briefly what
  is going on with the roof.
- The call is transferred to an independent, locally owned Houston-area roofing company, which
  hears a short summary before it picks up.
- If that company cannot answer, the caller's name, callback number, city, need and urgency are
  taken and passed along for a call back.

customer's insurer, as the customer arranges). The line never charges the customer. Companies may
pay the line a referral fee (always disclose this).

CertainTeed or any manufacturer program), rated or reviewed. Never name a partner company. Never
