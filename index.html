Do you offer free roof inspections in Houston?

Yes — free roof inspections for all Houston area homeowners. We'll do a thorough inspection and give you an honest assessment. If your roof doesn't need any work, we'll tell you that too.

Absolutely. We work directly with your insurance adjuster and help you navigate the entire claims process. Many Houston homeowners don't realize their insurance covers hail and wind damage.

Most residential roof replacements in Houston are completed in 1-2 days. Larger homes or complex roof designs may take 2-3 days. We'll give you a specific timeline during your free estimate.

We use premium shingles from GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed — all top-rated brands. We'll recommend the best material for your budget, climate, and home style.

What warranty do you offer?

All our installations come with a 25-year workmanship warranty plus the manufacturer's material warranty. Your investment is fully protected.
