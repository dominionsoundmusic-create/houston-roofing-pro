Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free roof inspections in Houston?

Call and you will be put through to a local roofer who can inspect the roof. Many roofers inspect storm damage at no charge, but confirm on the call, and ask for the findings in writing.

Do you help with insurance claims for storm damage?

A roofer can document the damage and meet your adjuster on site. In Texas, the claim itself is between you and your insurer. Read your policy for the deductible and whether it pays replacement cost or actual cash value.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Many residential replacements take a day or two, longer for large or complex roofs. Ask the roofer for the schedule before work starts.

What roofing materials do you use?

Most local roofers install major brands such as GAF, Owens Corning and CertainTeed. Ask about wind- and impact-rated shingles for Houston storms.

What warranty should I expect?

Warranties are set by the roofer who does the work, plus the shingle manufacturer's warranty. Get both in writing before you pay.
